Hello, and welcome. I’m so glad you’re here.

I’m Susan, a licensed clinical social worker and EMDR and IFS trained therapist based in Virginia. I work with children, teens, young adults, adults, and families, offering in-person sessions in my Alexandria office as well as virtual therapy for residents of Virginia and Utah.

At the heart of my work is a strong commitment to, and a deep belief in, each person’s capacity to heal, grow, and move toward a more grounded and fulfilling life, even when things feel overwhelming or uncertain.

Experience

Outpatient therapist for youth and adults

Endorsement in social work

A significant part of my career was spent in Salt Lake County, working with at-risk and under-resourced youth and families, and partnering with schools, behavioral health organizations, and state agencies. These experiences continue to shape how I show up in my work: with compassion, flexibility, and a deep respect for each person’s unique story.

My approach

Collaborative, practical, and deeply attuned to your individual needs. I draw from trauma-informed, evidence-based modalities, and together we’ll find what works best for you, whether that’s building coping skills, processing past experiences, or creating meaningful changes in your daily life.
